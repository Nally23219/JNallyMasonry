## Rules for these photos
- **CC BY 2.0:** the credit line (photographer, a link to the source, and a link to the license)
  must stay next to the photo. It's shown in the corner of each card on the site.
- **Public domain:** no credit is legally required, but we credit them anyway.
- Don't use any of these photos in a way that suggests the photographer endorses the company.
